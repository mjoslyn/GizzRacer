# GizzRacer: development notes

Browser kart racer built with Three.js. See [SPEC.md](../SPEC.md) and [MULTIPLAYER.md](MULTIPLAYER.md).

Play: https://www.gizzracer.com (Cloudflare Workers, also at gizzracer.mike-42e.workers.dev; deploys on every push to `master`). The old Netlify address redirects there (`netlify.toml`).

## Run

```sh
npm install
npm run dev       # http://localhost:5173
npm test          # vitest
npm run build     # typecheck + production build
npm run balance   # slow: all-CPU races on every track, wins and average place per racer
npm run dev:party # build, then run the game and its PartyKit servers locally on :8787 (wrangler dev)
npm run deploy    # build and deploy to Cloudflare (needs CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN and CLOUDFLARE_ACCOUNT_ID)
npm run seed:ghosts  # post CPU starter ghosts to every leaderboard (SEED_URL=https://www.gizzracer.com for live); rerun after a PHYSICS_VERSION bump
```

## Status

Milestones 1 to 7 are in (driving feel, the Rattlesnake greybox, CPU racers, items and hazards, menus and Grand Prix, the psychedelic art pass, all four Cup 1 tracks), a second cup of four tracks, plus twenty-one racers with their own vehicles (karts, a dog bed on wheels, a flying Gila monster, a flying pig, a fishing dinghy, a hovercraft, a levitating lotus, a toadstool saucer, a monowheel, a drum tank), the band among them (Amby, Cavs, Cookie, Jason on a lighting rig, Joey, Lukey and Stu), big-headed and in coveralls; twelve race at a time, the CPUs drawn at random with no repeats, themed worlds, and mobile play (touch controls, fullscreen, landscape). Online multiplayer (Milestone 8: Time Trial boards, rooms, live races) is in.

- Party mode: up to four on one screen racing a cup P1 picks as a Grand Prix (`src/ui/party.ts` join screen, `src/core/seatInput.ts` controls: a half of the keyboard, a gamepad each, or a phone). Two sit side by side, three and four take quarters (`src/render/split.ts`); each gets a camera, a HUD corner (`src/ui/seatHud.ts`) and an engine voice and one-shots panned to their side (`Sfx.at`). The solo player is the same code: one seat (`Seat` in game.ts) with the full HUD. Phones join by code or QR as controllers (`pad.html`, `src/pad.ts`, the `couch` party, docs/MULTIPLAYER.md section 12); Cast to TV opens the game on a Chromecast in Chrome
- Fixed 60 Hz physics with interpolated rendering (`src/core/loop.ts`)
- Keyboard + standard gamepad input as pure `InputFrame` data, auto-accelerate by default (`src/core/input.ts`), and smart steering that keeps you off the edges and straightens you out of turns (`src/physics/steerAssist.ts`)
- Arcade kart controller: per-racer stats (handling also sets how wide a kart skids through turns and drifts, `KartParams.slide`), engine classes, off-road penalty, fine steering (gradual ramp-in, response curve), hop, 3-tier drift boosts, ramps that launch off their lip, trick boosts, boost pads, walls you bounce off (square-on straight back, glancing along the wall; a graze only shaves speed) (`src/physics/kart.ts`)
- Track model: turtle-style builder (`src/tracks/builder.ts`), ribbons with per-section width, walls, surface and elevation, jump gaps over pits, branch shortcuts (`src/tracks/track.ts`)

- Track Generator (`src/tracks/generator.ts`): a mode in the picker that builds a new track at race time from three dials: theme (any of the forty worlds), twists (complexity 1 to 5: narrower roads, tighter corners, chicanes, switchback hairpins, jumps and more hazards) and length (short, medium, long). A seeded rectangle skeleton is filled with themed corner modules, each measured before it's placed, and closed exactly on the line; a layout that crosses itself is re-rolled from the same seed. Every track gets a generated name; after racing one you can keep it under that name or your own (`src/ui/saveTrack.ts`). Saved tracks are just seed and dials in the browser (`src/core/savedTracks.ts`), listed in the picker's Track row with a Delete button

- Lettering in Metal Mania (SIL OFL, `public/fonts`), after the PetroDragonic Apocalypse logo

- Elevation (SPEC section 7): every cup track climbs and dips like a roller coaster, with pillared decks, earth banks, sunk cuts (Rattle Canyon, Dragon's Gullet, the Ice V canyon) and crests you fly off; Fishing for Fishies's causeway dives under the water (with an underwater look, sound and sea life). The chase camera sits low and close behind, Mario Kart style
- Hazards on Rattlesnake (`src/items/hazards.ts`): snakes crossing the S-Bends, dust devils on the Salt Flat, tumbleweeds and falling boulders in Rattle Canyon. Their motion is a pure function of race time; CPUs steer around them and around mines
- Modes (`src/ui/menu.ts`): Grand Prix (pick one of the eleven album cups, in release order from the 12 Bar Bruise Cup to the Phantom Island Cup, or the Random Cup, four premade tracks drawn at random each time the picker lands on it (`randomCupTracks`, `chosenCup`), four tracks each, points 15/12/10/8/6/4, standings after each race, the leader starts at the back, a podium at the end; `src/core/grandPrix.ts`, `src/render/podium.ts`), Single Race, and Time Trial (one lap, alone, no items, best times kept in the browser, and your fastest run replays as a see-through ghost to race against; `src/core/ghost.ts`)
- Online Time Trial (Milestone 8a, docs/MULTIPLAYER.md): pick a display name in the picker, finished runs post to a leaderboard per track and class, and any listed run can be raced as a pink ghost next to your own. Servers in `party/` (PartyKit on Cloudflare Durable Objects): `players` (guest identities) and `trials` (boards and ghosts), with runs checked by `src/net/runCheck.ts`
- Online rooms (Milestone 8b): pick Online in the picker, create a room or join one by its 4-letter code or a `?room=` link. The lobby shows who's in, their racers and ping; the host picks track and class and starts once everyone is ready; CPUs fill the grid. Every client starts on the room's clock, and a pause from anyone pauses the race for everyone (anyone can resume). (`party/room.ts`, `src/net/room.ts`, `src/ui/lobby.ts`)
- Live online races (Milestone 8c): the room runs the race (CPUs, item boxes, items and hits) on a 60 Hz server loop and sends snapshots 15 times a second; each player drives their own kart locally and reports it, other karts are shown 0.1 s in the past, interpolated. Hits land on your kart as effects from the room, and the room calls the results (`src/net/roomRace.ts`, `src/net/onlineRace.ts`, `src/net/wire.ts`)
- Psychedelic layer (`src/render/postfx.ts`): heat haze that shimmers hardest on the straights (desert, and lighter on the volcano), the Rattlesnake sky turning a full circle of hue over the race, a kaleidoscope on the screen edges on the final lap pulsing with the music's beat, diamond-back barriers that crawl like scales, wheel dust off-road, and a camera flyover with a title card before each race
- Sound effects synthesized with Web Audio, no samples (`src/audio/sfx.ts`): engine pitched by speed with fuzz on boosts, tire squeal in drifts and hard turns, countdown, laps, drift sparks, hops, landings, walls, item pickups and uses, hits, and panned snake rattles and dust devil wind. The music sits under the effects and ducks for the big ones
- Soundtrack from seqbaby (account `gizzracer`), looping per track with a faster cut on the final lap, a title and menu theme in the manner of the long Ice V groove (7/8, a wandering bass line, Rhodes, flute), a pair each (lap and final lap) for the Apocalypse Cup's tracks (thrash for Dragon and Mars for the Rich, a storm groove for Supercell, synth prog with a robot narrator for The Lord of Lightning) the Phantom Island Cup's (orchestral, after the album) and the Omnium Gatherum Cup's (Magenta Mountain's dorian dance groove, Kepler-22b's swung electric piano and flute, The Garden Goblin's fingerpicked acoustic, Predator X's phrygian thrash), and a podium loop (`src/audio/music.ts`)
- Early-2000s look: procedural high-res textures, painted sky, lens flare, glossy karts, particle sparks, blob shadows, minimap; at High detail (`src/render/detail.ts`) sun shadows, bump-mapped roads and denser scenery
- Trackside detail (`src/render/trackDetail.ts`): painted edge and centre lines, a rubbered racing line that swings to the inside of each bend, themed edge posts and lights (fence posts, marker poles, torches, buoys, snow poles, neon street lamps, floating stars), chevron boards on sharp corners, a grandstand with a bouncing crowd and waving flags by the start, billboards and ground cover
- Scenery per theme (`src/render/trackView.ts`): saguaros, prickly pears, barrel cacti, skulls and hoodoos; fruit trees, hay bales, flower bushes and windmills; steaming lava pools, charred trees and basalt columns; giant flowers, glowing mushrooms and stone idols; stilt huts, rocking sailboats and gull rocks; igloos and snowmen; rooftop beacons and water tanks; floating islands and hot air balloons. A second, nearer ring of horizon silhouettes for depth

Flow: title, then a picker of four screens (mode, track or cup, racer, speed; `MenuStep` in `src/ui/menu.ts`, crumbs to jump between them). Grand Prix: race, results, cup standings, next race, and a podium after the fourth. Single Race and Time Trial: race, results, back to the picker.

Controls: auto-accelerate, arrows steer, Down brakes/reverses, Shift/Space hop+drift (tap at a ramp lip for a trick), E (or X, gamepad L1) item, Q look back, Esc pause. Touch: drag on the left half to steer, DRIFT, BRAKE and ITEM on the right, and tap the screen anywhere else to hop (tap at a ramp lip for a trick). Hop as the 1 goes out for a rocket start, and hop as a Nonagon Infinity reaches you to ride over it.

Credits (title screen, and a page of Settings): the song behind each track, the band's recordings the transcribed remixes were written from (`TRANSCRIBED` in `src/audio/songs.ts`), and what the game is made with (`src/ui/credits.ts`). Settings (title screen and pause menu): music and effects volume (races play the remix of the band's song each track is themed on, `src/audio/songs.ts`), automatic or manual throttle, and tilt steering for phones that have the sensor (turn the phone like a wheel; sensitivity, invert, recentre; it recentres at every race start, and the drag pad hides while it's on). The first tap on a phone with tilt asks drag or tilt, once. Phones: the first tap goes fullscreen and locks to landscape where the browser allows it (Android Chrome). Held upright, the game shows a "turn your phone sideways" card and pauses the race. iPhone Safari can't go fullscreen from a page, so the title suggests Share, Add to Home Screen; the web app manifest launches it fullscreen in landscape from there.

In a race: `R` restart (asks first; `R` or Enter again confirms, Esc keeps racing), `M` manual throttle, `N` music on/off, `B` sound effects on/off. In the picker, the racer screen shows the racer gallery, `R` picks a racer at random, and in Single Race `T` picks a random track. `?showroom` shows every racer on a turntable (`&racer=<id>&face` for a close-up of one driver's head). In the console, `gizz.warp(0.5)` jumps halfway round the lap and `gizz.autopilot()` lets the CPU drive your kart, `gizz.give('riffMissile')` fills the item slot.

## Soundtrack

The songs live in seqbaby. `public/music/*.mp3` are rendered from them by playing each song in headless Chromium through seqbaby's own engine:

```sh
# needs ../mjoslyn/seqbaby and ../seqbaby-seed checkouts (or SEQBABY_DIR / SEQBABY_SEED_DIR)
node tools/render-soundtrack.mjs            # all cues
node tools/render-soundtrack.mjs podium     # one cue
```

Some remixes are transcribed from the band's own recordings and written in seqbaby (`seqbaby-seed/gizzracer/remix-live.mjs`, the Nonagon Infinity Cup's in `seqbaby-seed/gizzracer/remix-nonagon-infinity.mjs`, and the Mind Fuzz Cup's, from Live at The Caverns '23, in `seqbaby-seed/gizzracer/remix-mindfuzz.mjs`); they render the same way.

The Polygondwanaland Cup's remixes and Polygondwanaland's are transcribed from the album masters (the band's free Polygondwanaland download) and written in `tools/polygondwanaland-cup-songs.mjs`, which exports `SONGS` in seqbaby-seed's shape (title, cue, bars, build) with each song's measured tempo, meter, key and section map in its comments. They belong in seqbaby-seed with the others; until they're moved there, render them through a seed folder whose `people.mjs` exports `PEOPLE = [{ handle: 'gizzracer', songs: SONGS }]`:

```sh
SEQBABY_SEED_DIR=/path/to/that/folder node tools/render-soundtrack.mjs tetrachromacy-remix
```

A new track's song needs live recordings too, for Track song and Random song: add it to `TRACK_SONGS` (`src/audio/songs.ts`), then run `NODE_USE_ENV_PROXY=1 node tools/bootlegs.mjs` and `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) NODE_USE_ENV_PROXY=1 node tools/bootleg-starts.mjs`. The first re-picks every song's recordings, so keep the songs that already had some (and their measured starts) and take only the new ones; `tests/bootlegs.test.ts` fails for a track whose song has none.

Every live recording starts on the music and ends with it: no crowd noise, tuning or stage banter at either end. `start` is where the music begins and `end` where it stops before the applause (the game loops or moves on there, `startLive` in `src/audio/music.ts`). For new recordings, download them and run `python3 tools/live-edges.py <file.mp3> ...` (needs librosa): it reads the low end, how well the harmony fits the song's key, and the beat, a second at a time, and prints the start and end with strips of the first and last minute to check them by. Keep a quiet intro or coda of the song itself, and a segue into the next song; trim the rest. Drop a recording the Archive won't serve, and a duplicate of one already in (the same release filed under two dates).

The 12 Bar Bruise Cup's remixes are transcribed from the live recordings the game streams for the cup and written in `tools/twelve-bar-bruise-cup-songs.mjs` on the same helpers; render them through a seed folder the same way. If a render stops with "recording shorter than one loop", the browser's audio fell behind real time: look for a section change that leaves a part ringing (Cut Throat Boogie's guitar going straight from 'four' into 'mono' did it) and reorder.

The Silver Cord Cup's remixes, from the soundboards the game streams for the cup, are in `tools/silver-cord-cup-songs.mjs` on the same helpers, on synths after the album (a siege kick, an acid bass, a Moog lead, the chants on a choir); render them through a seed folder the same way.

The Crumbling Castle remix plays on Crumbling Castle (`crumbling-castle-remix`, named by its song in seqbaby-seed's `gizzracer/remix-live.mjs`); Polygondwanaland's cue belongs to the title song above.
