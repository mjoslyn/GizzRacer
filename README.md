# GizzRacer

**Psychedelic kart racing in the Gizzverse.** A free kart racer that runs in your browser, on desktop and on phones, themed on the songs, albums and lore of King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard.

**Play now: [gizzracer.com](https://www.gizzracer.com)**

No install and no account. It will always be free. **[The manual](https://www.gizzracer.com/manual)** (also on the title screen) covers the modes, controls, moves, items, racers and tracks.

## What's in it

- **36 tracks in 9 cups**, one an album, in the order the albums came out, plus a Random Cup of four drawn from them
  - **Mind Fuzz Cup:** the suite that opens I'm in Your Mind Fuzz, in order: I'm in Your Mind, I'm Not in Your Mind, Cellophane, I'm in Your Mind Fuzz
  - **Nonagon Infinity Cup:** Robot Stop, Gamma Knife, People-Vultures, Wah Wah
  - **Flying Microtonal Banana Cup:** Rattlesnake, Open Water, Sleep Drifter, Doom City, all in the cover's cobalt and banana yellow
  - **Polygondwanaland Cup:** Crumbling Castle (on the N64 castle's layout), The Castle in the Air, Inner Cell, Tetrachromacy
  - **Infest the Rats' Nest Cup:** Planet B, Mars for the Rich, Perihelion, Hell
  - **Omnium Gatherum Cup:** Magenta Mountain, Kepler-22b, The Garden Goblin, Predator X
  - **Ice, Death, Planets, Lungs, Mushrooms and Lava Cup:** Ice V, Magma, Iron Lung, Gliese 710
  - **PetroDragonic Apocalypse Cup:** Motor Spirit, Supercell, Dragon, Flamethrower
  - **Phantom Island Cup:** Phantom Island, Deadstick, Panpsych, Sea of Doubt
- **Roller-coaster tracks:** climbs onto pillared decks, plunges you fly off, canyons sunk into the ground, and a causeway that dives under the lagoon
- **Launchers and gliders:** a glowing ramp on the lip of every track's big jump (and on a long straight where there's no jump) throws you high, and a trick off it opens your glider, so the leap is a glide you can steer. Up climbs (trading speed for height), down dives (picking speed up)
- **Rhythm humps, apex pads and slaloms:** a sharp crest on the long straights to fly off, a boost pad on the inside of the big hairpins for the tight line, and a slalom of three pads across the straights of the hillier tracks
- **Living tracksides:** every section's name on a board as you reach it, the grid painted on the road, stands of spectators at the sharpest bends, flocks of each world's creatures that scatter as you pass (vultures, gulls, bats, moths, drones, ghost lights), dust, pollen, snow, embers and spores in the air, and every light on the track pulsing on the final lap
- **Shortcuts on every track** that come and go by lap: a phantom causeway that only appears on lap 2, a snake that shuts its jaws, a tide that floods the boardwalk, a wall of fire that only drops on the final lap. Trick kickers on the straights and double item boxes on the risky lines
- **Hazards** from the songs: rattlesnakes, flame jets, tornadoes, black holes, dragons breathing fire across a gullet, planes gliding in with their propellers stopped, waves breaking over a causeway, garden goblins, geysers, a pliosaur snapping over the road's edge, castle masonry slamming down, gusts over the clouds, vesicles and prism beams in four colours, robots that stop dead in the road, a gamma knife's beams closing on one point, vultures diving at the road, wah pedals stamping down on it, smog rolling across a dead city, airlock doors sliding shut on Mars, solar flares arching over the road, rats pouring out of the pit, eyes that read your mind, rows of arrows with one not there, sheets of cellophane sliding past each other and patches of fuzz pulsing into the red. Use them well and they help: surf the wave, catch the fourth colour, hop onto a crashed plane for a ramp, ram a boulder on a boost, slip through a laser gate as it warms up, slip through the gamma knife's crosshair as it closes, hop as a vulture swoops and it carries you, slip through an airlock's slit, drive under a flare while it arches high, follow the eye's arrow, take the lane with no arrow, cross where the cellophane lines up, ride the fuzz while its needle is in the gold
- **24 racers**, each with their own vehicle: the Lizard Wizard, Han-Tyumi in a fishing dinghy, the Gila Rider's running Gila monster, the Gator's running alligator, a Skeleton Dragon, the Lord of Lightning on his bolt, a slithering Rattlesnake, Beowulf's Witch, the Fuzz Golem, Arlo the poodle in a dog bed, the Flying Pig captaining Flight b741, and more. The band are in it too: Amby, Cavs, Cookie, Jason, Joey, Lukey and Stu
- **Trophies and unlocks:** gold, silver and bronze per cup and class, shown on the cup picker. A trophy opens the Random Cup, a gold at 150cc opens **200cc**, and gold in every cup opens **Mirror mode**. Or open the game with **?unlock** on the address (www.gizzracer.com/?unlock) to unlock everything in that browser; ?unlock=off locks it back
- **Rivals:** after the first race of a cup the two racers nearest you in the standings become your rivals, named on the title card, ringed on the minimap and a touch sharper on the road
- **Style:** drifts, tricks, slipstreams, hazards used well, items landed and dodged, and shortcuts score as you race, chained when they come quickly, with a tally on the results and a best per track
- **14 items**, each with a Gizzverse name:
  - Mind Fuzz, Microtonal Banana, Nuclear Fusion, Big Fig Wasp, Horology, the Woo Cannon and The Dripping Tap, a long boost for the back of the field
  - a signature move for each band member, for example Stu's Sleep Drifter flute flurry or Cavs's Road Train
  - the Nonagon Infinity, a nine-sided ring that rolls down the road to the leader
- **Track Generator:** a new track from a seed, with your choice of world, twists and length. Save the ones you like. A **daily track** the same for everyone, with its own leaderboard
- **Leaderboards** for the fastest full race on every track (the daily included) and the best result in every cup, beside the picker, as well as Time Trial's
- **A soundtrack** of video-game remixes of the band's songs, one per track, with a faster cut on the final lap. Or the band live, from their shows on the Internet Archive's Live Music Archive (Sound on the picker's last screen, or Settings > Soundtrack): **Game sound** (the remixes), **Track song** (a live recording of each track's song, a new one every race), **Random song** (a live recording of any of their songs, a new one every race) or **Full set** (a whole show you pick, by date and venue or at random, played from the top as all the game's music, title and menus included, until you close the game). Live songs start where the band does, past their chat with the crowd, and every recording is evened up to the remixes' volume. A mini player (bottom left on desktop; on a phone a ♫ button that opens it) shows the song and the show, with back, pause, next (the next song of a set) and a link to it
- **A VHS look** after the Rattlesnake video: tape wobble and a duotone in each world's colours. You can turn it down or off.
- **Detail levels** (Settings > Detail; High by default, Low on phones): High has sun shadows from every kart, post, tree and stand, roads with grain that catches the light, and half again as much scenery; Medium keeps the resolution, the grain and nearby shadows with the usual scenery; Low draws fewer pixels with no shadows. On any level the resolution drops on its own while the game is dropping frames, and comes back once it isn't

## Modes

- **Grand Prix:** four races, points after each, rivals from the second race on, a podium at the end and a trophy to keep
- **Story:** Murder of the Universe as a roguelike run. Its three chapters (The Tale of the Altered Beast, The Lord of Lightning vs Balrog, Han-Tyumi and the Murder of the Universe) are each two races on new generated tracks (finish in the top four), a skill game, and a one-on-one duel with the chapter's boss: the Altered Beast in Inner Cell, the Balrog in Supercell, Han-Tyumi in Murder of the Universe. Every stage cleared gives a pick of three boons (Mind Fuzz, Rattlesnake, Sleep Drifter, the Gamma Knife and more). You have one life: a miss ends the run, unless Planet B has given you another, which restarts the stage. The skill games, a different one each chapter: **Play the Riff** (hit the notes as they cross the line), **Nonagon Infinity** (play back the lights round the nonagon), **The Flying Microtonal Banana** (tune a string to the drone until the wobble stops), **Horology** (stop the hand on the mark), **The Mind Fuzz Suite** (four quick movements, pass three), **Rattlesnake** (the classic snake game: eat the mice, grow, never bite your own rattle), **Robot Stop** (creep up on the robot while it looks away, freeze when it turns), **Fishing for Fishies** (strike when the bobber goes under, not on a nibble), **Crumbling Castle** (drop each sliding stone square on the tower, as what hangs over crumbles) and **Deadstick Landing** (glide off a track's launcher and land in the ring). Each skill game opens on a card of exactly what to do, with Continue to start (the Mind Fuzz Suite has one before each movement). The boon chooser shows your deck, every boon taken so far. Story races don't post times
- **Single Race:** any track, any class (50cc, 100cc, 150cc, and 200cc once it's unlocked), Mirror once that is
- **Time Trial:** one lap alone. Online leaderboards, one ghost to chase each race (the next one up the board from you, or one you pick; beat it and the next is next), and the gap to it at every checkpoint.
- **Between races:** the song plays on through the results and standings. Buttons there race on with the same song (S), go to the next race with its own song (N), or in Single Race take on the next track (T). In Story mode the same song plays on over the cards into the next race.
- **Posting online:** the first time you have a run, a race time or a cup result worth posting, the results screen asks for a name. Until then the leaderboards just show.
- **Infinite:** laps without end. **Switch tracks** never, every 1, 5, 10 or 20 laps, or with each song of a Full set, every track in turn; **Gen tracks** off (the game's own), on (a new generated one each time) or mix. Race the CPUs, or **Watch**: they race and you sit back, a visualizer for the music. The camera follows one racer and moves on now and then; left and right, or a tap, picks another
- **Online:** make a room, share the four-letter code or link with friends, and race live. CPUs fill the empty grid slots.

## How to play

| | Keyboard | Gamepad | Touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Steer | Left / Right (or A / D) | Left stick | Drag on the left half (or tilt the phone) |
| Accelerate | Automatic | Automatic | Automatic |
| Brake / reverse | Down / S | B | BRAKE |
| Hop and drift | Shift / Space | R1 | DRIFT, or tap anywhere |
| Use item | Up / W, or E | L1 | ITEM |
| Look back | Q | Y | |
| Pause | Esc | Start | Pause button |

- **Drift:** hold hop while turning. Sparks charge from blue to orange to purple, and letting go gives a boost. The longer you hold, the bigger it is. Hold a purple on through the corner for **rainbow** sparks, the longest boost there is.
- **Handling:** every kart skids a little wide through a turn, and wider in a drift. How wide is the racer's handling stat: a Fuzz Golem runs out toward the edge where Beowulf's Witch holds the line.
- **Tricks:** tap hop just as you leave a ramp, a crest or a launcher for a boost when you land. Chain them for bigger ones.
- **Gliding:** trick off a launcher (tap hop as you leave it) and your glider opens; without the trick it's just a big jump. Steer as on the road. Up climbs, at the cost of speed, until you stall; down (or brake) dives and picks speed up. On a gamepad it's the stick or d-pad, on touch a drag up or down the steering pad, or with tilt steering, tipping the phone back (climb) or forward (dive). With the glider open, up climbs rather than firing your item. It folds when you land, or after a couple of seconds.
- **Rocket start:** hop just as the countdown's 1 goes out.
- **Slipstream:** tuck in behind another kart to charge a boost.
- **Nonagon Infinity:** hop as it reaches you to ride over it. The warning's bar fills as it closes, and says HOP when it's time.
- **Random racer:** press R on the picker, or the dice beside the racer. In Single Race, T (or the dice beside the track) picks a random track. The picker is one choice to a screen: mode, track or cup, racer, speed; Enter goes on, Backspace back, and the crumbs along the top jump anywhere.

Smart Steering is on by default. It steers you back from edges and straightens you up out of turns, and you can always steer through it. On touch it holds on firmer, and the CPUs race a little slower. Turn it off under Settings > Steering.

**Hints ticker:** on the picker and over the flyover before a race, a line along the bottom scrolls through the controls (for keys or touch, whichever you're using), what every item does, and tips. Hover it to stop it.

**Turn hints** (on by default, Settings > Turn hints): a card at the top of the screen calls the next bend, an arrow curled the way it goes, how sharp (EASY, MEDIUM, SHARP, HAIRPIN) and how far off.

**CPU difficulty** (Settings > CPUs): Easy, Normal or Hard. Easy CPUs are slower, drift less and are careless with items; Hard ones are quicker and sharper. It applies offline, from the next race.

On phones, turn the phone sideways. On iPhone, use Share, then Add to Home Screen to play full screen.
