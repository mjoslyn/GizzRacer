# GizzRacer

**Psychedelic kart racing in the Gizzverse.** A free kart racer that runs in your browser, on desktop and on phones, themed on the songs, albums and lore of King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard.

**Play now: [gizzracer.com](https://www.gizzracer.com)**

No install and no account. It will always be free. **[The manual](https://www.gizzracer.com/manual)** (also on the title screen) covers the modes, controls, moves, items, racers and tracks.

## What's in it

- **28 tracks in 7 cups**, plus a Random Cup of four drawn from them, each built around an album or a song
  - **Nonagon Cup:** Mr. Beat, Rattlesnake, Magma, Polygondwanaland
  - **Microtonal Cup:** Fishing for Fishies, Ice V, Cyboogie, Catching Smoke
  - **Apocalypse Cup:** Mars for the Rich, Supercell, The Lord of Lightning, Dragon
  - **Phantom Island Cup:** Phantom Island, Deadstick, Panpsych, Sea of Doubt
  - **Omnium Gatherum Cup:** Magenta Mountain, Kepler-22b, The Garden Goblin, Predator X
  - **Polygondwanaland Cup:** Crumbling Castle (on the N64 castle's layout), The Castle in the Air, Inner Cell, Tetrachromacy
  - **Nonagon Infinity Cup:** Robot Stop, Gamma Knife, People-Vultures, Wah Wah
- **Roller-coaster tracks:** climbs onto pillared decks, plunges you fly off, canyons sunk into the ground, and a causeway that dives under the lagoon
- **Launchers and gliders:** a glowing ramp on the lip of every track's big jump (and on a long straight where there's no jump) throws you high, and a trick off it opens your glider, so the leap is a glide you can steer. Up climbs (trading speed for height), down dives (picking speed up)
- **Rhythm humps, apex pads and slaloms:** a sharp crest on the long straights to fly off, a boost pad on the inside of the big hairpins for the tight line, and a slalom of three pads across the straights of the hillier tracks
- **Living tracksides:** every section's name on a board as you reach it, the grid painted on the road, stands of spectators at the sharpest bends, flocks of each world's creatures that scatter as you pass (vultures, gulls, bats, moths, drones, ghost lights), dust, pollen, snow, embers and spores in the air, and every light on the track pulsing on the final lap
- **Shortcuts on every track** that come and go by lap: a phantom causeway that only appears on lap 2, a snake that shuts its jaws, a tide that floods the boardwalk, a wall of fire that only drops on the final lap. Trick kickers on the straights and double item boxes on the risky lines
- **Hazards** from the songs: rattlesnakes, flame jets, tornadoes, black holes, dragons breathing fire across a gullet, planes gliding in with their propellers stopped, waves breaking over a causeway, garden goblins, geysers, a pliosaur snapping over the road's edge, castle masonry slamming down, gusts over the clouds, vesicles and prism beams in four colours, robots that stop dead in the road, a gamma knife's beams closing on one point, vultures diving at the road and wah pedals stamping down on it. Use them well and they help: surf the wave, catch the fourth colour, hop onto a crashed plane for a ramp, ram a boulder on a boost, slip through a laser gate as it warms up, slip through the gamma knife's crosshair as it closes, hop as a vulture swoops and it carries you
- **24 racers**, each with their own vehicle: the Lizard Wizard, Han-Tyumi in a fishing dinghy, the Gila Rider's running Gila monster, a Skeleton Dragon, the Lord of Lightning on his bolt, a slithering Rattlesnake, Beowulf's Witch, the Fuzz Golem, Arlo the poodle in a dog bed, the Flying Pig captaining Flight b741, and more. The band are in it too: Amby, Cavs, Cookie, Jason, Joey, Lukey and Stu
- **Trophies and unlocks:** gold, silver and bronze per cup and class, shown on the cup picker. A trophy opens the Random Cup, a gold at 150cc opens **200cc**, and gold in every cup opens **Mirror mode**
- **Rivals:** after the first race of a cup the two racers nearest you in the standings become your rivals, named on the title card, ringed on the minimap and a touch sharper on the road
- **Style:** drifts, tricks, slipstreams, hazards used well, items landed and dodged, and shortcuts score as you race, chained when they come quickly, with a tally on the results and a best per track
- **14 items**, each with a Gizzverse name:
  - Mind Fuzz, Microtonal Banana, Nuclear Fusion, Big Fig Wasp, Horology, the Woo Cannon and The Dripping Tap, a long boost for the back of the field
  - a signature move for each band member, for example Stu's Sleep Drifter flute flurry or Cavs's Road Train
  - the Nonagon Infinity, a nine-sided ring that rolls down the road to the leader
- **Track Generator:** a new track from a seed, with your choice of world, twists and length. Save the ones you like. A **daily track** the same for everyone, with its own leaderboard
- **Leaderboards** for the fastest full race on every track (the daily included) and the best result in every cup, beside the picker, as well as Time Trial's
- **A soundtrack** of video-game remixes of the band's songs, one per track, with a faster cut on the final lap. Or the band live, from their shows on the Internet Archive's Live Music Archive (Sound on the picker's last screen, or Settings > Soundtrack): **Game sound** (the remixes), **Track song** (a live recording of each track's song, a new one every race) or **Live set** (a whole show you pick, by date and venue or at random, played from the top across races and menus, in any mode). Live songs start where the band does, past their chat with the crowd, and every recording is evened up to the remixes' volume. A mini player (bottom left on desktop; on a phone a ♫ button that opens it) shows the song and the show, with back, pause, next (the next song of a set) and a link to it
- **A VHS look** after the Rattlesnake video: tape wobble and a duotone in each world's colours. You can turn it down or off.
- **Detail levels** (Settings > Detail; High by default, Low on phones): High has sun shadows from every kart, post, tree and stand, roads with grain that catches the light, and half again as much scenery; Medium keeps the resolution, the grain and nearby shadows with the usual scenery; Low draws fewer pixels with no shadows

## Modes

- **Grand Prix:** four races, points after each, rivals from the second race on, a podium at the end and a trophy to keep
- **Single Race:** any track, any class (50cc, 100cc, 150cc, and 200cc once it's unlocked), Mirror once that is
- **Time Trial:** one lap alone. Online leaderboards, one ghost to chase each race (the next one up the board from you, or one you pick; beat it and the next is next), and the gap to it at every checkpoint.
- **Posting online:** the first time you have a run, a race time or a cup result worth posting, the results screen asks for a name. Until then the leaderboards just show.
- **Infinite:** laps without end on one track, or a **Tour** of every track in turn (the next every three laps, or with each song of a Live set). Race the CPUs, or **Watch**: they race and you sit back, a visualizer for the music. The camera follows one racer and moves on now and then; left and right, or a tap, picks another
- **Online:** make a room, share the four-letter code or link with friends, and race live. CPUs fill the empty grid slots.

## How to play

| | Keyboard | Gamepad | Touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Steer | Left / Right (or A / D) | Left stick | Drag on the left half (or tilt the phone) |
| Accelerate | Automatic (M for manual: Up / W) | Automatic (manual: A) | Automatic |
| Brake / reverse | Down / S | B | BRAKE |
| Hop and drift | Shift / Space | R1 | DRIFT, or tap anywhere |
| Use item | E | L1 | ITEM |
| Look back | Q | Y | |
| Pause | Esc | Start | Pause button |

- **Drift:** hold hop while turning. Sparks charge from blue to orange to purple, and letting go gives a boost. The longer you hold, the bigger it is. Hold a purple on through the corner for **rainbow** sparks, the longest boost there is.
- **Handling:** every kart skids a little wide through a turn, and wider in a drift. How wide is the racer's handling stat: a Fuzz Golem runs out toward the edge where Beowulf's Witch holds the line.
- **Tricks:** tap hop just as you leave a ramp, a crest or a launcher for a boost when you land. Chain them for bigger ones.
- **Gliding:** trick off a launcher (tap hop as you leave it) and your glider opens; without the trick it's just a big jump. Steer as on the road. Up climbs, at the cost of speed, until you stall; down (or brake) dives and picks speed up. On a gamepad it's the stick or d-pad, on touch a drag up or down the steering pad, or with tilt steering, tipping the phone back (climb) or forward (dive). With manual acceleration on, up stays the throttle, so it only dives. It folds when you land, or after a couple of seconds.
- **Rocket start:** hop just as the countdown's 1 goes out.
- **Slipstream:** tuck in behind another kart to charge a boost.
- **Nonagon Infinity:** hop as it reaches you to ride over it. The warning's bar fills as it closes, and says HOP when it's time.
- **Random racer:** press R on the picker, or the dice beside the racer. In Single Race, T (or the dice beside the track) picks a random track. The picker is one choice to a screen: mode, track or cup, racer, speed; Enter goes on, Backspace back, and the crumbs along the top jump anywhere.

Smart Steering is on by default. It steers you back from edges and straightens you up out of turns, and you can always steer through it. On touch it holds on firmer, and the CPUs race a little slower. Turn it off under Settings > Steering.

**Hints ticker:** on the picker and over the flyover before a race, a line along the bottom scrolls through the controls (for keys or touch, whichever you're using), what every item does, and tips. Hover it to stop it.

**Turn hints** (on by default, Settings > Turn hints): a card at the top of the screen calls the next bend, an arrow curled the way it goes, how sharp (EASY, MEDIUM, SHARP, HAIRPIN) and how far off.

**CPU difficulty** (Settings > CPUs): Easy, Normal or Hard. Easy CPUs are slower, drift less and are careless with items; Hard ones are quicker and sharper. It applies offline, from the next race.

On phones, turn the phone sideways. On iPhone, use Share, then Add to Home Screen to play full screen.
