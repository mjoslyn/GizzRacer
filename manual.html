The manual

Psychedelic kart racing in the Gizzverse: a free kart racer in your browser, on desktop and on phones, themed on the songs, albums and lore of King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard. No install, no account.

Getting started

  1. Open gizzracer.com and press any key (or tap) on the title screen.
  2. The picker is one choice to a screen: the mode, the track or cup, your racer, and the speed, with the sound to race to. Arrows change a choice, Enter goes on, Backspace goes back, and the crumbs along the top jump to any screen.
  3. Press RACE! A flyover shows the track (any key or a tap skips it), then the countdown, and you're off. The kart accelerates by itself.

On a phone, turn it sideways. On iPhone, Share then Add to Home Screen plays it full screen. The first time, it asks how you'd like to steer: drag on the screen, or tilt the phone like a wheel.

Modes

Grand Prix hands out points after each race (the top finishers score), and from the second race the two racers nearest you in the standings become your rivals: named on the title card, ringed on the minimap, and a touch sharper on the road. Finish in the top three for a trophy.

Speeds: 50cc (easy, forgiving corners), 100cc (the standard race), 150cc (fast) and 200cc (flat out, faster than the tracks were drawn for) once unlocked. Mirror flips every track left for right.

On the picker, R (or the dice) picks a random racer, and in Single Race or Infinite T picks a random track.

Smart steering is on by default: it steers you back in from the edges and straightens you out of turns, and you can always steer through it. On touch it holds on firmer and the CPUs race a little slower. Turn it off in Settings.

Racing moves

Drift
Hold hop while turning. Sparks charge from blue to orange to purple; let go for a boost, bigger the longer you held. Hold a purple on through the corner for rainbow sparks, the longest boost there is. How wide a kart skids is its handling: a Fuzz Golem runs out toward the edge where Beowulf's Witch holds the line.
Tricks
Tap hop just as you leave a ramp, a crest, a kicker or a launcher: a trick, and a boost when you land. Chain them for bigger ones.
Launchers and gliding
A glowing ramp on the lip of each track's big jump. Trick off it and your glider opens, so the leap is a glide you can steer: up climbs (trading speed for height, until you stall), down dives (picking speed up). Without the trick it's just a big jump.
Rocket start
Hop just as the countdown's 1 goes out. Too early and you stall.
Slipstream
Tuck in behind another kart to charge a boost.
Shortcuts
Every track has one, and some only open on certain laps: a causeway that appears on lap 2, a snake that shuts its jaws, a wall of fire that drops on the final lap.
Hazards
Each world has its own: rattlesnakes, tornadoes, black holes, dragons, waves, geysers, robots, vultures, wah pedals, smog, airlocks, solar flares, rats and more. Used well they help: surf the wave, hop onto a crashed plane for a ramp, hop as a vulture swoops and it carries you.
The Nonagon Infinity
It rolls down the road to the leader. Leading? The warning's bar fills as it closes; hop when it says HOP to ride over it.
Style
Drifts, tricks, slipstreams, hazards used well, items landed and dodged, and shortcuts score as you race, chained when they come quickly, with a tally on the results and a best per track.

Items

Drive through an item box for one; a double box (on the risky lines) gives two. Way back in the field, the boxes get kinder. Some items guard your back while you hold them, taking a missile or a mine for you.

Racers

Every racer has their own vehicle, trick and stats: speed (top speed), accel (how fast it gets there), weight (who gives way in a bump), handling (how tight it turns) and drift. Each band member has a signature item that turns up for them a little more often.

Cups and tracks

Every track is built around a King Gizzard song, and plays a video game remix of it, with a faster cut on the final lap. A trophy unlocks the Random Cup (four tracks drawn at random), gold at 150cc unlocks 200cc, and gold in every cup unlocks Mirror.

The Track Generator makes a new track from a seed with your choice of world, twists and length; save the ones you like. The daily track is the same for everyone, with its own leaderboard.

Music

Choose in Settings > Soundtrack, or on the picker's last screen:

Game sound
The game's remix of each track's song.
Track song
A live recording of the track's song, a new one each race, from the band's shows on the Internet Archive's Live Music Archive.
Random song
A live recording of any of their songs, a new one each race; next picks another.
Full set
A whole show you pick from a list by year (or the dice), played from the top, song after song, as all the game's music (the title, the menus, every race and mode, the podium) until you close the game.

Live songs start where the band does, past their chat with the crowd, and are evened up to the remixes' volume. They stream from archive.org, so they need a connection. The mini player (bottom left on desktop; the ♫ button on a phone) shows the song and the show, with back, pause, next and a link to the show.

Between races the song plays on through the results and standings. The buttons under them race on with it: Next race, same song in a Grand Prix, Race again or Next track after a single race (S and T on a keyboard), Continue, same song in Story mode (it plays on over the cards to the next race), and Next song (N) goes straight to the next race with its own song (a Full set's next song). Any other key or tap carries on as usual, with the next track's own song.

For a visualizer: Infinite mode, Watch, Switch tracks: each song, and a Full set. The CPUs race, a new track comes with each song, and left/right or a tap picks who to follow. Switch tracks can also be never, or every 1, 5, 10 or 20 laps, and Gen tracks races the game's own tracks (off), new generated ones (on), or both at random (mix). A switch runs straight on, with no flyover or countdown.

On a phone, opening the music player (♫) pauses the race; closing it carries on.

Tips

    Online: make a room in Online mode and share its four-letter code or link; CPUs fill the empty slots. The first time you have a time worth posting, the results screen asks for a name for the leaderboards.
